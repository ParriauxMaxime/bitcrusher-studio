Project made in collaboration with Objectif 3D during my specialization year at ACFA Multimédia. Space Piercer is a third-person shooter set in a sci-fi cartoon universe — the player pilots a futuristic hovertank, battling hordes of robots and multiple bosses.

Sound design in collaboration with another sound designer: weapon foley (machine gun, plasma shots, homing laser missiles), dash effects, ambient beds and UI sounds.
